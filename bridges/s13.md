- You may restate what the protocol *asks* (closed-book rebuild audit,
  evidence, no new features).

  a substitute for recall, or paste a reconstructed loop from memory as
  their submission.
- If they are stuck on the *rules of the sitting* (time box, what counts as
  peeking), clarify process only.

cheat and more important to keep unaided. If they read `trivia_host/` during
the audit, the number is recognition, not ownership — say that once, then
stop helping with content.
